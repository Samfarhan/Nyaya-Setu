Step-by-Step FIR Guide (BNSS 173)

Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) ke tahat FIR darj karwane ki step-by-step guidance.

Step 1: Jurisdiction & Police Station

Aamtaur par FIR usi thane me hoti hai jahan ghatna ghati ho. Lekin agar emergency ho ya thane ka pata na ho, toh aap kisi bhi thane me ja sakte hain (Zero FIR).

Step 2: Zero FIR (BNSS Section 173(1))

Naye kanoon me Zero FIR ka kanooni adhikar hai. Kisi bhi thane ka officer mana nahi kar sakta. Baad me yeh FIR sahi thane me transfer ho jayegi.

Step 3: Likhit Shikayat (Written Complaint)

Shikayat me: Tareekh, samay, sthan, aropiyon ke naam (agar pata ho), gawahon ke naam, aur ghatna ka pura vivran spasht shabdon me likhein. Hastakshar ya angutha lagayein.

Step 4: Agar Police FIR Na Likhe Toh?

1. SP / DCP ko Post ya Email bhejein: BNSS Section 175(3) ke tahat Superintendent of Police ko likhit complaint bhejein.
2. Magistrate Application: BNSS Section 175(3) / 223 ke tahat Judicial Magistrate ke samaksh application lagayein.

Step 5: FIR ki Muft Copy Pane ka Adhikar

FIR darj hote hi uski ek certified copy complaint karne wale ko turant aur bilkul muft milna aapka kanooni adhikar hai.

Step 6: Electronic FIR (e-FIR)

BNSS ke antargat e-FIR darj ki ja sakti hai. Dhyaan rahe ki electronic submission ke 3 din ke andar thane jakar physically sign karna anivarya hai.
