> **Empowering Indian Citizens with Accessible, Empathetic, and Accurate Legal Guidance.**

Nyayi is an advanced Indian legal intelligence assistant designed to democratize legal literacy across India. Powered by high-speed neural models and comprehensive knowledge of Indian statutory frameworks, Nyayi translates complex procedural law and statutes into clear, actionable, citizen-friendly explanations.
